# Car_Sales_Analysis-Dashboard

# Business Problem
Automobile businesses generate large volumes of sales data across different vehicle models, dealers, and regions. Without proper visualization, identifying performance trends and sales opportunities becomes challenging.This dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights.
# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
KPI	Value
YTD Total Sales	$371.2M
MTD Total Sales	$54.28M
YTD Average Price	$28.0K
MTD Average Price	$28.26K
YTD Cars Sold	13.3K
# Key Insights
Total YTD sales exceeded $371M.
More than 13,000 vehicles were sold.
Sales performance varied significantly across dealer regions.
Certain body styles contributed a larger share of revenue.
Interactive filtering allows deeper analysis of sales patterns.
# Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of car sales performance across multiple regions, vehicle categories, and dealers. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor sales trends, evaluate dealership performance, and identify business growth opportunities.
